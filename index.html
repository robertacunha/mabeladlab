Approach

How I build an ad set

The same four steps for any brand. The ads below are what the steps produce once the buyer, the levers and the test plan are agreed.

01

Learn the buyer first

Audience, price band and product range before the first headline. Here that is owners of hair salons, barber shops, nail studios, beauty clinics, massage studios and physio practices in the Netherlands who answer their own phone and chats between clients, choosing between Mabel Solo at €49 a month and Mabel Team at €89.

02

Pick the lever, then write

Every ad keeps the brand's existing tone of voice and adds one psychological lever and two emotional triggers to it: contrast, mental simulation, risk reversal, the IKEA effect. The copy follows the lever, so each variant tests a separate idea.

03

Build the system once

Four layouts, one palette, one badge. Each layout has its own visual style: a 1950s rotary phone on navy for awareness and Stories, a coral field for offers, ruled cream paper for consideration, a printed appointment card for the ask, and a navy booking slip for retargeting. Within a round, the layout and the screen style stay fixed, so the message is the only thing a test reads.

04

Test one variable at a time

Each round has one variable, one primary read and one guardrail. The winning message carries forward and the business types rotate, so every segment gets read.

Method

How a round is run

Meta A/B test for every round

Meta splits the audience so cells never overlap. Flexible ads mix creative on their own and hide which element won, so they come in after a winner exists.

Seven to thirty days per round

Seven days is the floor, so weekday and weekend behaviour both count. The round closes once each cell reaches its conversion target, within 30 days. Results in the first days are unstable, so no winner is called before the round closes.

Even budget, enough results

Cells share the round budget evenly. A winner is called once each cell has roughly 25 to 50 of its target event.

Winner carries forward

The next round holds the winning message and changes the next variable. Every new idea is tested against the current winner.

Optimise on what Meta can learn from

Before round 1, the pixel and Conversions API are checked against real trial signups, with consent mode set for EU traffic. Meta's delivery learns once an ad set reaches about 50 optimisation events in a week. Until paid subscriptions reach that, rounds optimise on trial starts, and paid subscriptions are read as the result.

Call winners on confidence

Meta reports how likely the winning cell is to win again. A low score counts as a tie, and the current winner stays. From round 5 a winner must also keep cost per paying subscriber within the payback target on gross margin.
